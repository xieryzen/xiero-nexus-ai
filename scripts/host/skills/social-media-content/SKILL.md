# Social media content

A post earns a second of attention and has to pay it back. Give one useful thing; ask for one action.

## 1. Before writing

- The account's audience, in one line, and what they come for.
- The goal of this post: reach (new people), trust (people who know the account), or sale (people ready to buy). One goal a post.
- The facts available from the brief. No invented prices, results, reviews or statistics; a gap is `TODO(client)`.

## 2. A carousel (five slides is a good default)

1. **Cover**: the promise in at most seven words, large. It must make sense alone in a feed.
2. **Slides 2 to 4**: one point each. A short headline, one or two lines, and something to look at. Each slide stands alone when it is shared.
3. **Last slide**: the summary in one line and the one action (save, send to a friend, order, visit).

Rules:

- Words on a slide: a headline of five to seven words, body of 20 words at most. If it needs more, it is two slides or a caption.
- Text large enough to read on a phone without zooming; high contrast; the same margins, type and colours on every slide.
- Square pictures (1:1). Leave a calm area in each picture where the text will be set: say where ("upper third empty"). Generated pictures carry no lettering; the words are added afterwards from your slide text.
- The first and the last slide carry the name or handle, small.

## 3. A single post

- One picture that shows the thing, one line on it at most.
- Caption: the first line is the point (everything after is hidden behind "more"); then the detail the picture cannot say; then the action.

## 4. Captions

- Write as the account talks. Short lines, a blank line between thoughts.
- Specifics: a price, a date, a place, a size.
- One call to action, matching the goal: "Simpan dulu buat nanti" (reach), "Tanya stok via DM" (sale).
- 3 to 8 hashtags that the audience searches: the subject, the city or niche, the brand. None that are only popular.
- No engagement bait ("tag 3 friends to win") unless it is a real, legal giveaway with terms.

## 5. A content calendar

For each entry: date, format, goal, the working title, the one point, the picture idea, the action. Mix the goals over a week: mostly useful or interesting, some trust, few sales. Leave room for what happens.

## 6. Deliverable

For a carousel:

```
Goal: ...
Slide 1 - headline: ... | body: ... | picture: ... | where the text sits: ...
...
Caption: ...
Hashtags: ...
Alt text: one line for each slide, for people who cannot see it
First comment / pin: ...
```

## 7. Check

- Would a stranger understand the cover without context?
- One point a slide, one action a post?
- Is every claim in the brief?
- Read the caption's first line alone: is it the point?
