# Vertical short video

People decide in the first second and a half. Most watch without sound. Plan for both.

## The first two seconds

The hook is one of these, said and shown at once:

- the result ("Kamar 3x3 ini jadi studio dalam sehari"),
- a specific mistake ("Tiga salah setting yang bikin server lag"),
- a number that surprises, or a question the viewer already has.

Not a greeting, not the brand name, not "Hai guys". The picture shows the subject from the first frame.

## Shape (about 20 to 30 seconds)

1. Hook (2 s): the promise.
2. Two or three beats (5 to 7 s each): one point each, each with something new to look at.
3. Pay-off: the promise kept, shown.
4. Call to action (3 s): one thing to do ("Simpan untuk nanti", "Link di bio").

One idea a video. If it needs "and also", it is two videos.

## Frame and text

- 9:16. Keep faces, products and words in the middle band: the top is covered by the app's header, the bottom third by the caption and buttons, the right edge by icons.
- Subtitles for every spoken word, two lines at most, large, high contrast, in the middle band.
- On-screen text: five words or fewer a beat. It repeats the key word, not the whole sentence.
- Pictures are generated square and cropped to vertical: the subject is centred with margin; nothing important near the left and right edges.

## Lines to speak

- Conversational, the way the audience talks. Short sentences. About 2.3 words a second.
- Each line can stand alone: viewers join late.
- No filler openers; no "jangan lupa like, comment, share" before anything was given.

## Caption and hashtags

- First line: the hook again in other words (it is all that shows before "more").
- Two or three lines of what the video does not say: a detail, the price, where.
- One call to action.
- 3 to 8 hashtags: two broad, the rest specific to the subject and the place. No wall of tags, none unrelated to the video.
- A posting note: best first comment, and what to pin.

## Honest limits

- Claims come from the brief. No invented results, prices or reviews.
- No trending audio is chosen for the client: music rights differ by account type. Say that a sound should be picked in the app.
- No real people's likenesses and no brand marks that were not supplied.

## Check

- Cover the sound: does it still work?
- Is the first frame the subject?
- One idea, one call to action?
- Read every line aloud with a timer.
